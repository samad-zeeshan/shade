# Pitch, 90 seconds

Each line gives the time, what to do on the page, and what to say.

**0:00. Page open, tab 1, time at 07:00.**
Say: This is a construction site in Dubai on a July day. Each dot is a work spot. Blue areas are in the shadow of a building. The colour of a dot shows the heat index at that spot. Orange is hotter.

**0:15. Press play. Let the clock run to 13:00.**
Say: Watch the shadows move. At noon the site has almost no shade. The heat index at some spots goes over the limit for heavy work. At other spots, in shade, a crew can still work.

**0:30. Stop at 13:00. Hover a dot in the shade.**
Say: The tooltip shows how many minutes a crew can work at this spot in this hour. It also shows when work is next permitted here. The numbers come from two peer-reviewed models. One gives the heat index. One gives the body-heat limit.

**0:45. Click tab 1, Blanket ban.**
Say: Today the law stops all outdoor work from 12:30 to 15:00. Before and after the ban, crews work in full sun. The first tile shows the result. Crews spend 38 hours over the heat limit. That is not safe.

**1:00. Click tab 2, Heat limits, one site temperature.**
Say: Now apply the heat limits with one temperature for the whole site. Nobody goes over the limit. But the crews need 10 hours of overtime. Some work moves to a night shift. That costs more and needs lights.

**1:10. Click tab 3, Heat limits, per-spot shade.**
Say: Now the plan knows the shade at each spot for each 15 minutes. Nobody goes over the limit. Overtime drops to 30 minutes. Fifteen of 16 tasks finish inside the shift. Per-spot shade cuts overtime and night work by 95 percent. Look at the timeline. Each worker moves between spots as the shadows move.

**1:25. Click Sources.**
Say: Every number on this page points to a source in this list. The plan runs for any date with live weather from Open-Meteo. On a wet July morning the gain is smaller. Shade helps most when the sun, not the wet air, sets the heat.

**1:30. Close.**

The numbers above come from the committed `web/data.json`. The solver stops at 60 seconds, so a rebuild on another machine can shift them a little. Read the tiles before you speak.
