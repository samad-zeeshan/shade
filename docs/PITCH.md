# Pitch, 90 seconds

Each block gives the time, what to do on the page, and what to say. The spoken text is about 220 words.

**0:00. Home page, top of the page, then scroll to The problem.**
Say: The law stops outdoor work in the Gulf from 12:30 to 15:00. We tested whether that ban is enough. We used real weather from hot summer days at two sites, one in Dubai and one in Abu Dhabi. It is not enough. With the ban alone, workers at the Dubai site still spent about 29 to 39 hours a day over the safe heat limit, added up across the crew. That is not safe.

**0:25. Scroll to How it works.**
Say: Our tool finds where building shadows fall at each work spot, every 15 minutes. It turns the weather and the shade into a heat limit for each spot. Then it plans who works where, and when.

**0:41. Scroll to Results.**
Say: Planning by heat fixes the problem. Workers stay under the limit. On most days the extra time is under one hour. That is a good result. On one humid day both plans need about 11 extra hours, because humid air limits work even in the shade.

**0:53. In the results table, select Dubai in the row for 28 June 2026. In the planner, press Play the day, then press 1, 2 and 3 to switch between the three plans.**
Say: Shade helps most on the hardest day, 28 June 2026. The heat plan treats every spot as if it is in full sun, and it needs about three and a half hours of overtime. The shade plan uses the shade at each spot. It needs about one hour, and it finishes three more tasks inside the working day. That is a clear gain. On the milder days the gain is under an hour, so shade is a small edge and not a big one.

**1:24. Back to the home page.**
Say: Next we add more cities, and a live plan each morning from that day's forecast.

**1:30. Close.**
